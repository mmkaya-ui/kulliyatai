# Sample corpus: sources and licensing

These 5 PDFs let anyone run the project and the evals without the private
corpus. They were generated by [`scripts/build_sample_docs.py`](../scripts/build_sample_docs.py)
from plain-text public-domain sources. Each PDF has a `- N -` page footer, so
citations ("page N", 1-based) can be checked by eye.

| File | Language | Pages | Work | Source | Why it is free to redistribute |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tr_teskilati_esasiye_1924.pdf` | Turkish | 7 | Teşkîlât-ı Esâsîye Kanunu (1924 Constitution of Turkey, final 1960 amended text) | [tr.wikisource](https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/Te%C5%9Fk%C3%AEl%C3%A2t-%C4%B1_Es%C3%A2s%C3%AEye_Kanunu_(1924)) | Official legal text, excluded from copyright under Turkish law (FSEK; Wikisource license tag `FSEK-32`) |
| `tr_omer_seyfettin_hikayeler.pdf` | Turkish | 9 | Ömer Seyfettin, "Kaşağı" and "Başını Vermeyen Şehit" | [tr.wikisource](https://tr.wikisource.org/wiki/Ka%C5%9Fa%C4%9F%C4%B1) | Author died in 1920: public domain worldwide (life + 70) |
| `en_us_constitution.pdf` | English | 9 | U.S. Constitution, original 1787 text (**no amendments** in this edition) | [Project Gutenberg #5](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5) | U.S. federal government work |
| `en_scandal_in_bohemia.pdf` | English | 16 | Arthur Conan Doyle, "A Scandal in Bohemia" (1891) | [Project Gutenberg #1661](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1661) | Author died in 1930: public domain worldwide |
| `de_kafka_die_verwandlung.pdf` | German | 29 | Franz Kafka, "Die Verwandlung" (1915) | [Project Gutenberg #22367](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/22367) | Author died in 1924: public domain worldwide |

Notes:

- The Project Gutenberg header and footer (and with them the "Project Gutenberg"
  trademark) are stripped. That is what PG asks for when the bare
  public-domain text is redistributed.
- The PDFs in the repo were rendered with Arial on Windows. Rebuilding with
  another font (e.g. DejaVu in Docker) can shift page breaks, and then the
  expected pages in `evals/questions.jsonl` would need re-checking. Rebuild only
  if you also re-validate the eval set.
- The corpus is multilingual on purpose (TR / EN / DE), so the evals also test
  cross-document and cross-language retrieval.
